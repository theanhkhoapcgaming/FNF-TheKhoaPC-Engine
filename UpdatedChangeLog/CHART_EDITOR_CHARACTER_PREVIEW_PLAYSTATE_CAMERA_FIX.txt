Chart Editor Character Preview - PlayState Camera Fix

- Character JSON position[] is applied once to the preview character, matching PlayState.startCharacterPos().
- Character JSON scale is preserved; preview no longer scales characters down to fit the window.
- Preview camera scroll is calculated from Character.getMidpoint() and the same role-specific offsets used by PlayState.moveCamera():
  - Opponent: midpoint.x + 150 + camera_position[0]
  - BF: midpoint.x - 100 - camera_position[0]
  - Both: midpoint.y - 100 + camera_position[1]
- position[] is never reused as a camera offset.
- Animation offsets are not injected into the camera target.
- Camera viewport continues to follow its draggable preview window and is clipped to the window content area.
