Chart Editor UI update
=====================

- File / Edit / View are now accessible from a fixed top toolbar styled after Character Editor.
- Clicking a top toolbar button selects the matching existing PsychUIBox tab and opens its panel.
- The Information box is now a fixed, compact horizontal panel at the bottom of the screen.
- The Information panel no longer uses the previously saved floating position; it is anchored to the bottom edge.
- Existing File/Edit/View tab contents and their actions are retained.
