Chart Editor Character Preview - Center Lock v6

- Character preview sprites use scrollFactor (1, 1).
- The preview camera scroll is recalculated after super.update() every frame.
- The camera target is the center of the rendered sprite bounds, adjusted for
  the current animation offset, and is placed at the center of the preview area.
- Character world position is held at (0, 0); the camera performs the centering.
- This avoids mixing viewport-local sprite positioning with camera scrolling.

This patch has ZIP integrity validation only. It has not been Haxe-compiled or
runtime-tested; verify camera scroll sign/offset behavior in the target Flixel build.
